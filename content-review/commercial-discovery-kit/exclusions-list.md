# Exclusions List

内部 draft / discovery use only

## Excluded Now

- live case-by-case individual advice
- hiring, firing, demotion, discipline, return-to-work final decisions
- medical or diagnostic interpretation
- legal advice or final compliance judgment
- crisis, self-harm, abuse, acute safety response
- unlimited meetings or unlimited revisions
- white-label resale rights
- open-ended custom implementation labor

## If These Come Up

- high-risk employment or accommodation matters:
  escalate to human review boundary
- medical or diagnostic interpretation:
  do not answer inside package scope
- crisis or safety:
  immediate external escalation
- requests for unlimited support:
  out of package scope
