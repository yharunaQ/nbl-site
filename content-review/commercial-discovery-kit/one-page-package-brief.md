# NBL OS Pilot

内部 draft / discovery use only

## What It Is

`NBL OS Pilot` は、AI-native social OS の最小導入単位。
人の常設相談窓口を外注するものではなく、`issue structuring`、`workflow mapping`、`private knowledge layer`、`boundary design` を導入する pilot である。

## Best Fit

- design-forward employer
- employer-facing intermediary
- repeated use を想定できる team

## Included

- AI team startup
- private workspace setup
- issue and workflow map
- knowledge pack initialization
- boundary and escalation pack
- bounded agent usage entitlement
- first operating review memo

## Wrapper Options

### Workplace Pilot

- 1 organization
- 1 named sponsor
- 1 primary workflow lane

### Partner Node Pilot

- 1 intermediary team
- 1 named operational owner
- 1 network-facing workflow lane

## Not For

- live case-by-case individual advice
- final employment decisions
- medical or diagnostic interpretation
- unlimited support
- white-label resale

## Next Step

discovery call で、fit、boundary、repeated use potential、owner clarity を確認する。
