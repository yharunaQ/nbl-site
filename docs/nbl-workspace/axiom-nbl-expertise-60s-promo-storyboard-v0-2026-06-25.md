## Source Basis

This draft is grounded in the current `NBLの専門性` page role:

- NBL's expertise is not simple search or summary.
- The first layer is expertise in how to read information without closing it into diagnosis, blame,制度名, or quick answers.
- The second layer is an expert knowledge network that holds person, work, environment, support, time, and institution together.
- AI is used to separate observation, hypothesis, counter-hypothesis, missing context, and "cannot yet say."
- Public surfaces should translate reviewed knowledge into consultation cases, design guides, articles, diagrams, and tools.
- Strong expertise includes clear stopping rules: no individual judgment, medical judgment, legal judgment, employment decision, or guaranteed outcome.

## Creative Direction

- Format: 60 seconds, 16:9 horizontal master. Can be cropped later to 9:16 with safe center composition.
- Visual tone: public trust, warm intelligence, AI-native but not sci-fi.
- Palette: warm off-white, jade, deep slate, muted coral, quiet blue accents.
- Style: editorial infographic illustration with cinematic motion design; no stock-photo feel.
- Human depiction: abstract, dignified, non-identifying; avoid stereotypes of disability or illness.
- Text in generated stills: avoid embedded readable text; add final captions in video editing for accuracy.
- Motion language: fragments gather, lines connect, cards separate, paths translate into tools, boundary gates close and open deliberately.

## 60-Second Scenario

### 0-10s: Problem

- Visual: A quiet desk surrounded by floating fragments: manuals, search snippets, survey sheets, workplace notes, and policy-looking documents. A person is not drowning, but clearly carrying too much context at once.
- Narration: `障害者雇用や難病就労支援の情報は、たくさんあります。けれど、断片を集めるだけでは、働きづらさの全体像は見えません。`
- On-screen copy: `情報はある。全体像が、まだ見えない。`
- Motion: Fragments drift in from different directions and fail to align.

### 10-20s: NBL's Reading Layer

- Visual: The fragments pass through a calm analytic lens and reorganize into six fields: person, work, environment, support, time, institution. Keep labels for video overlay, not image generation.
- Narration: `NBLは、情報をそのまま答えにしません。人、仕事、環境、支援、時間、制度の関係として、もう一度読み直します。`
- On-screen copy: `答えにする前に、関係として読む。`
- Motion: Fragments slow down, rotate, and connect into a balanced map.

### 20-30s: Hypothesis, Not Final Judgment

- Visual: Five translucent cards form a line: observation, hypothesis, counter-hypothesis, missing context, cannot-yet-say. A human reviewer silhouette stands beside the cards; AI is shown as a light or sorting layer, not a robot judge.
- Narration: `AIの読解力は、断定のためではなく、仮説、反対仮説、足りない文脈、まだ言えないことを分けるために使います。`
- On-screen copy: `AIは判断者ではなく、候補を分ける補助線。`
- Motion: One glowing path splits into multiple cards, then pauses at the human review point.

### 30-40s: Knowledge Network

- Visual: Domestic research, practice records, manuals, workshop notes, and international public information become a layered knowledge network. The network links to work density, recovery time, information access, disclosure boundary, support continuity, and evaluation.
- Narration: `読み取った知識は、専門知識ネットワークとして持ちます。資料の主張を丸のみせず、何を照らし、何を落としているかを見ます。`
- On-screen copy: `知識を集めるだけでなく、偏りごと読む。`
- Motion: Separate source streams become nodes and edges, with some edges marked as review-required or hold.

### 40-50s: Public Interfaces

- Visual: The network translates into visible public surfaces: consultation case readings, work/social participation design guide, reports, diagrams, and toolkit cards. A small group around a table uses these artifacts, not a chat window.
- Narration: `その知識を、相談事例、設計ガイド、記事、図解、ツールへ翻訳します。複雑な全体像を、人が使える入口に変えるためです。`
- On-screen copy: `専門知識を、人が使える入口へ。`
- Motion: Network nodes unfold into article pages, guide cards, diagrams, and workshop materials.

### 50-60s: Boundary And Invitation

- Visual: A calm public site interface sits inside a clear trust frame. Around it are boundary markers: no medical judgment, no legal judgment, no individual employment decision, human review. The final frame leaves room for the NBL logo or site URL.
- Narration: `強い専門性とは、言い切る強さだけではありません。どこで止め、人間が何を確認するかまで設計すること。Next Being Labは、働きづらさを仕事条件の地図へ変えていきます。`
- On-screen copy: `Next Being Lab / 働きづらさを、仕事条件の地図へ。`
- Motion: Boundary frame locks into place, then the central path opens toward the site.

## Boundary Review

### Asset Identity

- Asset: `NBLの専門性` page-based 60-second promotional video storyboard
- Source / real-asset status: derived from current next NBL public-candidate page copy and internal page role; no individual case or sensitive material used
- Publication status: internal draft only, not approved for public release

### Intended Use

- Audience: initial public-site visitors, supporters, enterprise / policy / research collaborators, internal reviewers
- Intended action stimulus: understand NBL as a knowledge-network and work-design reading method, then continue into site pages or collaboration review
- Surface: short web / SNS / site intro video candidate

### What This Can Convey

- NBL is not a simple search, summary, diagnosis, or FAQ product.
- NBL's value is a disciplined reading method and expert knowledge network.
- AI helps structure candidates and missing context; it does not own final judgment.
- Public materials translate complex knowledge into human-usable forms.

### What This Must Not Replace

- Medical judgment
- Legal judgment
- Individual employment or accommodation decision
- Public approval or source/support validity review
- Human professional review

### Likely Misunderstandings

- Viewers may think NBL provides individual相談 or AI判定.
- Viewers may read the source-network visual as official certification.
- Viewers may interpret "専門知識ネットワーク" as closed doctrine rather than reviewed, revisable knowledge.

- Short site note: `この動画はNBLの考え方の紹介です。個別相談、医療判断、法的判断、就職・配慮の判定を行うものではありません。`
