## Fit Or Drift Indicators

- Fit: named operational owner, recurring review or escalation willingness, repeated-use demand, and low automation pressure.
- Fit: workflow redesign appetite, a clear sponsor, and willingness to hold a boundary around final employment judgment.
- Fit: research or policy partners that help translate the market, but do not expect them to own operational intake.
- Drift: diagnosis-only framing, labor-outsourcing expectation, public-good rhetoric that wants unlimited support, or one-off advice only.
- Drift: treating a lighthouse partner as the operational review owner.
