## Purpose

This addendum records the Founder correction and external second-opinion advice for the Axiom-based next NBL site.

Falcon final-site page purposes, layout rhythm, visual assets, and navigation roles may be reused as delivery scaffolding. Falcon public copy is not Axiom core truth. Concrete page substance must be rebuilt from the Founder-accepted Axiom integrated domain knowledge object.

This document is not publication approval, public navigation, source/support validity, runtime, prompt, retrieval, model/provider, DB/schema, candidate_pattern promotion, or learning update.

## Page Requirements

### Home

Do not start with abstract mission prose. Start with a concrete object the reader can understand quickly: a scene, a case-reading turn, or a high-impact visual. The purpose sentence can follow.

### Scene Entry

Keep NS-04 as an independent page. It should use 4-koma scenes, short points, and use-case examples to show how a visible problem is reread as work conditions. It must not be folded into the toolkit.

### Consultation Case Reading

The FAQ catalog is a static reconstruction of an intended dynamic consultation function. Therefore comprehensiveness matters. Use the 42 semantic facets and residual watchlist as a coverage guard, while keeping raw/sensitive data out of public drafts.

Comprehensiveness must mean selectable consultation entry, not textbook volume. The first reader-facing experience should accept fragmented, plain-language questions from workers/families, employers/managers, supporters, medical/welfare/education actors, administration/training/policy actors, and social-communication actors. It should let the reader choose a near fragment and a near problem field, then immediately show:

- the current consultation phrase
- the likely over-narrow reading to avoid
- a wider work-condition reading
- an alternative hypothesis
- missing-context questions
- one small next move

The tone must be kind and practical. It must not make readers feel they are being lectured, dismissed, or told that only experts can understand the issue. Internal terms such as kernel, Axiom findings, review units, and coverage audits must not lead the public-facing body.

The FAQ catalog and model case readings should not appear as separate textbook chapters after the consultation tool. They are functions inside the consultation finder: problem-field options preserve FAQ coverage, and the selected role / fragment / problem field should automatically surface the nearest model case reading. Do not add a fourth "choose a case directly" step unless a future runtime-like interaction explicitly requires it.

The consultation page hero should not add explanatory card stacks under the hero image. The title and lead should say, in reader-facing language, that a fragmented consultation can start here; do not lead with Axiom naming, internal method explanation, or expert-sounding self-description.

The structure should remain future-runtime-friendly as data, but no runtime/API implementation is part of this slice.

### Work-Design Views Guide

The 10 findings and 37 substructures are internal review units, not a reader-facing table to paste into the site. Add a reader re-editing layer:

- situation levels from problem state to better work-design state
- practical labels and examples
- merge/split/rename freedom
- no fixed 21-view or 27-seed count

### Articles

Use a three-depth reading design:

1. infographic or key visual that conveys the point quickly
2. skimmable headings and short blocks
3. deeper explanatory body for readers who want the full argument

Article topics should come from high-leverage social/work questions as read through Axiom integrated knowledge.

The article page must not read like information-provider explanation. Each article should begin from reader-side problem awareness: disabled / chronically ill workers and families, employers and managers, supporters, medical / welfare / education actors, administration / training / policy actors, and wider policy or social debates. From that reader question, the article should use the Axiom integrated knowledge network to identify the smallest credible route toward solving the social problem: what is being misread, which work / participation conditions need to be seen together, what can be tried first, what remains uncertain, and which related case / guide / tool helps next.

The target is not neutral content supply or opinion quantity. It is a logical, public-readable, "worth spreading" article: close enough to the reader's real friction to feel usable, broad enough to change the social frame, and disciplined enough not to become individual advice, legal / medical / employment judgment, or source/support validity approval.

Falcon / NBL's SNS auto-post accumulation may be used when it already contains useful article seeds. The usable parts are: short-question phrasing, recurring role / friction clusters, internal product-return labels, and Image-2.0 post-card visual metaphors. The unusable parts are: reaction counts, impressions, raw comments, DMs, individual post authors, current-policy assertions from headlines, or anything that would treat a post as evidence. In the public article page, this should appear as "short questions that grew into articles", not as SNS operations, automation, or reaction analysis.

The public-candidate article implementation must behave like a real article product, not a one-page list of cards. Each full article must include: a reader-side hook, one visual / infographic entry, body headings that are themselves skimmable, body sections, old-reading vs work-condition-reading contrast integrated near the title rather than isolated as an internal panel, one first move with its practical reason, discussion questions framed as meeting / consultation prompts, and article-specific next-use groups. Do not add a redundant "skim heading list" when the body headings can do that work. Do not add an internal-looking explanation of the visual when the visual can carry the point. Next routes must be more specific than generic page links: each article should show near consultation examples, relevant work-design domains, and concrete toolkit selections before linking to the corresponding page / anchor. After Falcon NS-05 and work-condition virtual-forum articles were reread, the current implementation uses a selectable 36-article reader rather than vertically stacking every essay. The 36 catalog entries are the canonical article set; already-polished essays are reused, and the remaining entries are expanded into full article objects from each reader-side question, work-condition argument, first-use move, theme, audience, and route. Article visuals must be article-specific Image-2.0-style illustrated infographics, not abstract SVG diagrams, category-common images, or mismatched SNS cards. Each visual should show the reader's concrete friction, the work-condition reframing, and the practical route back to consultation / design / tools in one glance, with Japanese text allowed when it improves picture/text integration.

The 36-entry article catalog is now the top article-selection interface for the 36 full articles, not a production backlog, not 36 long articles stacked in one scroll, and not a separate bottom "article list" after the reader. The catalog has two complementary indexes: (1) Falcon-derived / virtual-forum-level public themes such as employment quality, invisible illness, treatment and work, mental health, diversity and participation, support connection, interaction, accommodation and work design, multidisciplinary collaboration, workplace contact points, implementation and training, triadic perspectives, policy and research, materials and meetings, and organization/support; and (2) reader-facing work / participation conditions: health time, information access, workplace operation, disclosure / evaluation, entry / transition, and support / institution. The page must be searchable and filterable in the static page by high-level theme, work-condition entry, audience, and short problem words, without adding runtime search, DB search, retrieval, or SNS operations. Those filters should directly narrow the full-article selector, and the selected article should open in the same reader. A separate lower article list and a separate "short questions to articles" block should not appear in the visible public-candidate page; the short-question / SNS-origin material is background for article generation, not a reader-facing extra chapter. Article visuals should not be cropped as thumbnails: the selected article shows a wide visual immediately before the body, and enlargement must happen in an in-page overlay so the reader does not navigate away from the selected article or return to article 1.

### Toolkit

The toolkit is not a dumping ground for the scene page. It gathers non-text-heavy ways to reduce cognitive load: diagrams, shared visual objects, worksheets, workshop formats, scripts, audio/video concepts, and multimodal teaching tools.

### Condition Window

Disability category and disease-name entrances are legitimate reader needs. The page should accept that entry path, then move away from lookup logic toward time, information, mobility, communication, evaluation, support, and workplace-condition questions.

### Method / About

Full boundary and trust explanation should concentrate here. Other pages should use progressive disclosure and short boundary cues instead of repeating heavy problem/promise/not-this blocks everywhere.

## 2026-06-12 Consultation Case Collection Implementation Note

The consultation case-reading page must preserve Falcon NS-02's product role: a broad consultation-entry catalog plus a visible reasoning process. It should not be a generic set of internal Axiom cards.

The current Axiom consultation page therefore uses a dedicated public-candidate body with:

- a process panel that turns a short consultation sentence into an observation / stuck reading / work-condition reading / counter-hypothesis / confirmation map;
- stakeholder entry cards for fragmented questions from the worker/family, employer/manager, supporter, medical/welfare/education side, administration/training/policy side, and research/social communication side;
- a broad FAQ catalog spanning health time, information participation, procedure changes, movement/tools, disclosure, evaluation/growth, support retranslation, and pre-entry/transition;
- model consultation readings that show the consultation sentence, common stuck reading, work-condition reading, counter-hypothesis, missing context, what changes when more information is added, and a small next trial;
- a fragmented-question path that lets users start before their question is well organized, while keeping the page from becoming individual advice or a deterministic answer table.

This keeps the static public-candidate page usable as a future dynamic-consultation scaffold while staying within delivery projection. It is not individual consultation, public approval, publication, runtime, source/support validity, or learning update.

## 2026-06-13 Work-Design Views Guide Implementation Note

The work-design guide must preserve Falcon NS-03's role as a future-oriented guidebook, not a consultation FAQ and not an internal review ledger.

The current Axiom work-design guide page therefore uses a dedicated public-candidate body with:

- a hero that frames the page as moving from difficult work situations toward designable conditions;
- a page-level introduction to situation levels, plus per-view situation-level strips modeled on Falcon's `WorkDesignSituationLevelStrip`;
- 10 work-design views derived from the Founder-accepted Axiom integrated-domain knowledge object;
- 37 concrete check items exposed as expandable detail under the relevant view;
- reader-facing labels and examples instead of Axiom object ids, L3 seed names, or fixed old 21-view language;
- audience-use cards for workers/families, employers/managers/HR, supporters/medical/welfare/education actors, and administration/training/regional collaboration.

Each Axiom view should show its own observation axis and four practical levels in problem-to-solution order: `破綻・停止`, `高頻度支障`, `要調整`, `安定・予防`. This keeps the guide from becoming a list of abstract findings and lets readers see how a concrete problem situation can move toward a more workable design state.

The visible page may mention the 10 views and 37 check items as a practical guide structure, but it must not present them as a final official taxonomy, a direct L3/Falcon copy, or a completed public approval. It remains an internal public-candidate projection from reviewed Axiom knowledge, not actual public navigation, publication, runtime, source/support validity, or learning update.

## 2026-06-21 Work / Social Participation Master-Plan Revision

Founder review found that the NS-03 candidate was still too close to an internal "10 views + 37 check items" ledger. The public-candidate page must instead read as a future-oriented guide for designing work and social participation in the AI era.

The visible page name is now unified as `未来の仕事・社会参加設計ガイド`. The route slug may remain `work-design-views-guide` for compatibility, but reader-facing copy should not oscillate between `視点ガイド`, `仕事設計ガイド`, and `未来の仕事設計ガイド`.

The guide's public role is:

- to move from disability-employment minority insights toward majority-facing work / social-participation design;
- to show a broad scope covering management, employment administration, professional support, training, institutional design, and regional collaboration;
- to present a master plan rather than a list of internal Axiom units;
- to use visual structure first, then detailed text.

The current public-candidate body groups the 10 Axiom-derived design points into five reader-facing design domains:

1. `就職前・入口・移行`
2. `健康時間・生活保障・仕事密度`
3. `情報・手順・接触点`
4. `開示・評価・役割・成長`
5. `支援・制度・知識更新`

The 37 lower items should be called `具体設計項目`, not merely `確認項目`. They are design parts that show what work / participation conditions need to be built, not a thin checklist.

The page should use strong visual assets before explanatory text so readers can grasp the whole guide visually before reading details. The 2026-06-21 HTML/CSS reproduction attempt clarified that code-native maps and cards do not carry the same perceptual meaning as Image-2.0 editorial illustrations. Therefore the page uses generated bitmap visuals as public-candidate design assets where they materially improve comprehension, while typed data and HTML remain responsible for accessibility, navigation, tests, and source-of-truth structure.

The opening `全体地図` and `状況レベル` sections should not read like abstract prefaces. The opening map must show the relation among person, work, life, health, information, support, and institution before the reader reaches the detailed cards. The situation-level overview must not present the four levels as a linear step ladder. It should present them as simultaneous situation lenses for work / participation design: responding to visible breakdown, catching repeated friction early, adjusting concrete conditions, and designing preventive / growth-oriented conditions. The five design-domain tiles are navigation controls, not decorative summaries: each tile links to its corresponding lower domain section.

The 2026-06-21 image-first visual pass fixed the Hero duplication by replacing the split text/HTML-map composition with a generated hero image saved at `public/images/axiom-work-design-guide/work-social-participation-hero-v1.png`. It also places generated representative cards for `健康時間` and `職場アクセス` at `public/images/axiom-work-design-guide/health-time-design-card-v1.png` and `public/images/axiom-work-design-guide/worksite-access-design-card-v1.png`. These images may contain Japanese text because Image-2.0 handles this use case well and separating picture/text can weaken comprehension. The images are visual/public-candidate assets, not source truth, public approval, or replacements for the Axiom-derived typed content structure.

The follow-up pass extends that image-first rule to all 10 work/social-participation design points. The page now connects each typed Axiom design point to a generated public-candidate 4-stage card under `public/images/axiom-work-design-guide/`, while keeping the typed HTML substructure for accessibility, route tests, and correct text. The generated cards are allowed to integrate Japanese labels and illustrations when that makes the situation levels easier to grasp than separated picture/text blocks.

The section-card order was then corrected so each design point starts with reader comprehension rather than abstract interpretation. The two generated visual assets must stay adjacent, because separating similar visual blocks with explanatory text makes the card boundaries harder to read. Each card now follows:

1. title and short explanation;
2. `図解1｜具体設計項目` as an Image-2.0 illustrated board for the section's 3-6 lower design parts;
3. `図解2｜状況レベル4コマ` as the generated staged situation card;
4. `視点転換のポイント`, including the `絡まり -> 設計` frame and `古い読み / 設計の読み / 目指す状態`;
5. `設計ポイント`, with three bullets for the whole design point and two concrete design bullets for each of the 37 lower items.

This order is intended to let readers understand the concrete design parts and the stage progression as one visual sequence, then read the conceptual shift after the image-based grasp has formed, and finally convert that understanding into usable design checks. The exact substructure labels and explanations remain in typed HTML for accessibility and regression tests; the generated boards are visual/public-candidate comprehension assets.

This remains a delivery-layer projection from reviewed Axiom integrated-domain knowledge. It does not approve public release, actual public navigation, publication, runtime, source/support validity, or learning update.
