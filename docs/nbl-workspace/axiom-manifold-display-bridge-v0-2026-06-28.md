## Goal

Founder noted that splitting Axiom knowledge into easy NBL site pages reduces cognitive load, but can make the core expert knowledge network feel like a black box.

This slice creates a bridge artifact with two directions kept separate:

- reality direction: a latent manifold / invariant structure takes diverse expression forms under different conditions
- human-observation problem: people perceive only partial and biased shadows of those expression forms, then build concepts, systems, and support practices around those shadows
- reconstruction direction: Axiom uses ICF interaction framing and LLM reading to reconstruct a reviewable invariant-structure candidate from those shadows and biased language
- delivery direction: the NBL site re-expresses that restored structure as human-readable pages, diagrams, articles, tools, and video candidates

The phase order on the screen starts from the material humans actually have in hand:

1. information fragments are shown as distorted `shadows`
2. ICF/LLM reconstruction shows how the shadows are related back to person / job / environment / support / time / institution
3. Axiom's reviewed structure appears as a latent invariant-structure candidate
4. public pages, diagrams, videos, and toolkit materials are shown as reviewed human-readable expression forms

The purpose is understanding and review, not public persuasion yet.

## 2026-06-28 Ontology Direction Correction

- observable reality is that invariant structure taking many expression forms under different conditions
