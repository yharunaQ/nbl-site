# Axiom Scene Entry Old-New Issues Redefinition v0

Date: 2026-06-23

Lane: Falcon Lab / Axiom delivery projection

Status: internal candidate design note. This does not approve publication, runtime behavior, source/support validity, or learning update.

## Purpose

The `scene-entry` page should not be a list of ordinary workplace scenes.

Its role is to show the old-but-new problems that NBL-Axiom exists to address:
problems that have been named for years in disability employment, reasonable accommodation, treatment-and-work, diversity, support coordination, training, policy, and AI/search literacy, but remain difficult because the relevant relationships exceed ordinary human cognitive load.

The page should therefore present use cases at the level of structural social problems, then use 4-koma manga to make each problem concrete.

## Definition Of An Old-New Issue

An issue qualifies as `old_new_issue` only when all five conditions hold.

1. It has been recognized repeatedly in practice, policy, training, or public discussion.
2. It remains hard to solve because it requires holding multiple context layers at once.
3. It is easily flattened into a familiar but insufficient answer.
4. It can be re-read as work / participation conditions rather than a person-only or制度-only problem.
5. NBL-Axiom can help by reducing cognitive load: separating observations, hypotheses, missing context, counter-hypotheses, actor conditions, and communication routes.

Issues that are merely frequent workplace scenes, common consultation topics, or Axiom internal knowledge categories should not be used as the top-level eight.

## Cognitive Load Dimensions

The eight issue cards should be checked against these load dimensions.

- `actor_load`: person, company, manager, supporter, medical/welfare/education, administration, policy, and public discourse do not share one map.
- `translation_load`: diagnosis,制度, guideline, support menu, or source text must be translated into work conditions.
- `time_load`: symptoms, treatment, recovery, workload, evaluation, livelihood, and transition unfold on different timelines.
- `visibility_load`: visible numbers and labels hide role, participation quality, evaluation, growth, and informal burdens.
- `source_load`: searches, AI summaries, public materials, SNS reactions, and official-looking texts contain partial views and bias risk.
- `reuse_load`: good individual handling remains tacit and person-dependent instead of becoming reviewable design units.
- `learning_load`: consultation, training, policy, article, tool, and SNS learning do not naturally circulate back into better public knowledge.

## Redefined Eight Issues

### 1. 見える数字と、見えにくい参加

Public problem:
雇用率や採用数は見えるが、役割・評価・成長は見えにくい。

Why the cognitive load is high:
Numbers are easy to compare, while participation quality requires seeing role, evaluation, growth, health time, support connection, and review opportunity together.

Common flattening:
雇用率、採用数、定着率が上がれば成果が出ていると読む。

Axiom re-reading:
Visible employment numbers are treated as an entrance, not the answer. The work condition map asks what role, evaluation, growth path, health-time freedom, and consultation line are actually available.

Use-case focus:
Policy / company reporting moves from count achievement to participation quality design.

### 2. 名前で止まる

Public problem:
診断名・障害種別・配慮名から答えを探してしまう。

Why the cognitive load is high:
Names are useful for indexing and access, but actual support depends on the interaction among task, commute, workload, information form, evaluation, support actor, time, and preference.

Common flattening:
同じ診断名なら同じ配慮が必要、または配慮名を選べば答えが出ると読む。

Axiom re-reading:
Diagnosis and disability category are kept as important information, but they are routed through work-condition questions before any interpretation is used.

Use-case focus:
The page should show why NBL is not a disease-to-accommodation lookup.

### 3. 健康時間

Public problem:
通院・治療・回復・症状変動が勤務表や評価に翻訳されにくい。

Why the cognitive load is high:
Workload, deadline, treatment, symptom fluctuation, recovery, income anxiety, and evaluation period move on the same calendar but are usually managed in separate vocabularies.

Common flattening:
休むか頑張るか、体調管理ができているか、配慮するかしないかの二択にする。

Axiom re-reading:
Health time is treated as a work condition: workload density, recovery margin, treatment schedule, sharing boundary, backup route, and evaluation timing are placed on one temporal map.

Use-case focus:
Treatment-and-work and rare/chronic disease issues become a general design problem about time, not a private weakness.

### 4. 情報の分断

Public problem:
本人、企業、医療、福祉、教育、行政が同じ仕事条件の地図を共有しにくい。

Why the cognitive load is high:
Each actor has useful information, but no actor naturally owns the translation from that information into concrete work conditions. The worker often carries the explanation burden.

Common flattening:
情報提供、連携、申し送り、相談窓口があればつながると読む。

Axiom re-reading:
Information is not considered connected until it has been translated into who needs to know what, for what work condition, at what timing, with what boundary.

Use-case focus:
NBL makes fragmented expertise usable without pushing the whole translation burden back onto the person.

### 5. 制度から現場へ

Public problem:
合理的配慮や制度説明が、作業・手順・相談線・評価運用へ落ちにくい。

Why the cognitive load is high:
Legal and制度 language is necessary but too high-level to directly operate tasks, schedules, tools, information forms, review timing, and workplace communication.

Common flattening:
制度を説明すれば現場で実装できる、または制度語だけで正解が決まると読む。

Axiom re-reading:
制度語 is preserved but translated into observable workplace conditions: time, task, information, environment, support, evaluation, and review route.

Use-case focus:
The page should show how legal / policy terms become concrete work-design questions without pretending to provide legal judgment.

### 6. 上司依存

Public problem:
理解ある上司や個別対応に乗り、再利用できる設計単位にならない。

Why the cognitive load is high:
Good local handling often contains valuable tacit knowledge, but it is distributed across people, timing, informal trust, memory, and exception handling.

Common flattening:
理解ある人がいればうまくいく、または個別対応は属人的だから仕方ないと読む。

Axiom re-reading:
The point is not to blame goodwill. The point is to recover the reusable design unit: procedure, record, sharing scope, support connection, review trigger, and replacement route.

Use-case focus:
NBL turns good handling into a reviewable and teachable work-design object.

### 7. 検索・SNS・AI要約の限界

Public problem:
検索や要約は入口になる一方、古い障害観や単純化を再生産しうる。

Why the cognitive load is high:
Public sources and AI summaries can be useful, but they mix partial views, recency differences, institutional assumptions, popularity signals, and bias risks.

Common flattening:
分かりやすい検索結果、AI要約、SNS反応、公式-looking資料をそのまま説明や根拠にする。

Axiom re-reading:
Sources are read as partial views. The system separates source position, what is visible, what is missing, bias risk, and the next work-condition question.

Use-case focus:
NBL uses AI reading power to reduce bias reproduction rather than amplify it.

### 8. 学びが育たない

Public problem:
研修・政策・相談・記事・SNSの問いが、改稿や教材化の循環に残りにくい。

Why the cognitive load is high:
Questions, misunderstandings, silence, useful examples, and objections appear across many surfaces, but they are rarely returned to a shared knowledge structure.

Common flattening:
研修は研修、相談は相談、政策議論は政策議論、SNSは発信で終わると読む。

Axiom re-reading:
Social interaction is not treated as popularity proof. It becomes material for reviewing misconceptions, missing context, article gaps, diagram needs, consultation cases, work-design guides, and toolkit updates.

Use-case focus:
NBL shows an AI-native public knowledge loop without treating reaction metrics or comments as evidence by themselves.

## Mapping To Axiom Knowledge Units

The eight old-new issues are reader-facing entrance problems.

They should not be confused with:

- the 15 / 18 kernel review units, which are reasoning and review discipline;
- the 10 Axiom integrated knowledge candidates and 37 substructures, which are domain knowledge structure;
- Falcon L3 21 principal patterns + 6 axes, which are bootstrap semantic prior;
- specific workplace scenes, which are concrete examples used inside the 4-koma.

Each old-new issue may draw from multiple Axiom knowledge units.

## Page Implication

The `scene-entry` page should be structured as:

1. Hero: eight old-new issues as structural problems, not ordinary work scenes.
2. Selector grid: the eight issue names and one-line public problem.
3. Each use case:
   - old-new issue title;
   - why it remains hard;
   - common flattening;
   - Axiom / NBL re-reading as work conditions;
   - 4-koma manga that makes the issue concrete;
   - link to case readings, work-design guide, article/toolkit route when available.

The 4-koma should not merely illustrate one workplace episode. It should show the transition from flattened public/common reading to a shared work-condition map.
